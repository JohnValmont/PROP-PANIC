Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no email sign-up, no cookies, no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking pixels, no payments.
  • The nickname you type is shown to the other players in your room. It is not saved on our server after you leave. Please don't use your real name.
  • Your level, coins, cosmetics and settings are kept in your own browser, not on our server.
  • For play statistics we record a random ID your browser makes up, plus how long you played and how many rounds. It is not linked to your name or IP address.
  • Your IP address is used only in memory, to stop abuse. The game does not write it to logs or a database.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads.

3. What is stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember things between visits. This data stays on your device. Only your nickname, level and cosmetic look are sent to the server when you join, plus the random browser ID described above.

  • Your profile: level, XP, coins, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, feats.
  • Settings: nickname, volume, music, mouse sensitivity, field of view, quality, key bindings, help and notices you have dismissed.
  • Session helpers: a short-lived seat token so a dropped connection can rejoin the same seat, and the random browser ID used for statistics.

To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings. That resets your progress, and we cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.

4. Services we rely on

  • Hosting: the game runs on Render. Like any host, Render processes your IP address to deliver the site and may keep request logs under its own policy.
  • Statistics database: visit records and round results (section 2) are stored with Supabase. Only the game server can read or write them.
  • Fonts: the page loads the Fredoka font from Google Fonts. Your browser requests it directly from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy. The game still works if the font is blocked.

We do not sell or rent personal information, share it for advertising, or use it for profiling.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. The server checks every message, applies rate limits, and gives out reconnect tokens that are random and expire quickly. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold small so there is little to lose.
