How to play

If you are a PROP

  1. Become an object. Aim at any object and press the Become key (E).
  2. Hide in plain sight. Stand among objects like you and stop moving.
  3. Don't flinch. Freeze when the blue scanner ring passes. Moving reveals you.
  4. Got hit? You get a speed burst. Run, drop a decoy (Q), turn ghost (F).
  5. Feeling brave? Taunt (T) near hunters for points, or jump on a hunter's head to stun them.

Survive until the timer hits zero.

If you are a HUNTER

  1. Wait blindfolded while the props hide.
  2. Zap the fakes. Left click fires. Three hits catch a prop.
  3. Don't guess. Every innocent object you shoot drains Focus. At zero you become a duck.
  4. Use your tools. Scanner (Q) pings props that move. Shockwave (F) throws nearby fakes into the air.
  5. Listen. All props squeak every 22 seconds, and the meter warms up when one is close.

Catch every prop before time runs out.
