How to play

If you are a PROP

  1. Become an object. Aim at one and press E (or BECOME).
  2. Blend in. Stand among objects like you and keep still.
  3. Got hit? Run. Three hits and you are caught.

Survive until the timer hits zero.

If you are a HUNTER

  1. Study the room while the props hide. Whatever appears later is a fake.
  2. Zap the fakes. Three hits catch a prop.
  3. Do not guess. Real objects drain your Focus. At zero you turn into a duck.

Catch every prop before time runs out.

Caught? You come back as a ghost. Aim at any object and click to rattle it: if a hunter shoots it, you score.
