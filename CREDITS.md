# Credits

Every model, texture and HDR panorama in `client/public/art` comes from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and is
released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain). No attribution is
required; this list exists so each file can be traced back to its source, and as thanks to the artists and to
everyone who funds Poly Haven.

The files are fetched and optimised by `tools/assets.mjs` from the list in `tools/asset-list.mjs`
(meshes simplified and meshopt-compressed, textures resized to WebP).

## Everything else

The hunter robot, the blaster, trees, grass, water, signs and rugs are generated in code. All sound effects and
music are synthesised at run time. The interface font is [Fredoka](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka)
(SIL Open Font License), loaded from Google Fonts.
